one much-loved Ramayana tradition

When the lamps
welcomed Rama home.

Diwali is celebrated in many ways. This familiar story from the Ramayana is one cherished explanation for the rows of lights.

01 — THE EXILE ✧

Fourteen years away

Prince Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana leave their home in Ayodhya and spend fourteen years in exile.

02 — THE BATTLE ⚔

Ravana is defeated

Ravana, the king of Lanka, takes Sita away. Rama and his allies travel to Lanka; Rama defeats and kills Ravana in battle, and Sita is freed.

03 — THE RETURN ⌂

Home to Ayodhya

With the exile complete, Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana return to Ayodhya, where their people are waiting to welcome them.

04 — THE LIGHTS ✦

A city of diyas

People light rows of lamps to celebrate the homecoming—a glow remembered as hope and good overcoming evil.

✦ This is one popular telling; Ramayana traditions and Diwali customs vary across regions and families.
